Ben Bioren

senior at UW, BS/MS (MS in June 2028)

Theorem 1 (about). I’m a senior at the University of Washington in the combined BS/MS program, studying computer science and math (BS in Computer Science, BA in Mathematics) with a minor in philosophy. I’ll finish my MS in Computer Science in June 2028.

My research is on making multimodal and reasoning models more reliable. In the RAIVN Lab, I study why vision-language models hallucinate and how to detect it at inference time. With Sewoong Oh, I work on typographic adversarial attacks on VLMs and defenses against them. In the Math AI Lab, I build tools that help LLMs write formal proofs in Lean 4. Before that, I spent almost two years in the Department of Radiation Oncology with Sunan Cui and Jie Fu, building machine learning models for cancer outcome prediction.

This past summer I was an ML kernel engineering intern at Annapurna Labs (AWS), writing quantized transformer kernels for Trainium. Before that, I was a GenAI alignment intern at Scale AI. I’m also a TA for the Allen School and occasionally write about visual proofs.

If you have similar interests, or would like to talk more about other things, please feel free to reach out! My email is bbioren@uw.edu.
