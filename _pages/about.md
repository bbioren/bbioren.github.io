**Theorem 1** _(about)._ I'm a senior at the [University of Washington](https://www.washington.edu/) in the combined BS/MS program, studying computer science and math (BS in Computer Science, BA in Mathematics) with a minor in philosophy. I'll finish my MS in Computer Science in June 2028.

My research is on making multimodal and reasoning models more reliable. In the [RAIVN Lab](https://raivn.cs.washington.edu/), I study why vision-language models hallucinate and how to detect it at inference time. With [Sewoong Oh](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~sewoong/), I work on typographic adversarial attacks on VLMs and defenses against them. In the Math AI Lab, I build tools that help LLMs write formal proofs in Lean 4. Before that, I spent almost two years in the [Department of Radiation Oncology](https://www.radiationoncology.uw.edu/) with [Sunan Cui](https://www.radiationoncology.uw.edu/faculty-sunan-cui/) and [Jie Fu](https://www.radiationoncology.uw.edu/faculty-jie-fu/), building machine learning models for cancer outcome prediction.

This past summer I was an ML kernel engineering intern at Annapurna Labs (AWS), writing quantized transformer kernels for Trainium. Before that, I was a GenAI alignment intern at Scale AI. I'm also a TA for the Allen School and occasionally write about [visual proofs](/blog/).

If you have similar interests, or would like to talk more about other things, please feel free to reach out! My email is `bbioren@uw.edu`.
